🔑 KeySmith Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

✅ Zero Data Collection

The Short Version

KeySmith does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your data. Ever.

Data Collection

KeySmith collects zero data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reporting to external services
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs or tracking code

Data Storage

All passwords and settings are stored locally on your device using the iOS Keychain, protected by the Secure Enclave. Your data never leaves your device.

Network Access

KeySmith makes zero network requests. The app requires no internet connection and has no server-side component.

Keyboard Extension

The KeySmith keyboard extension generates passwords locally. It does not log keystrokes, transmit data, or access any information beyond what is needed to insert generated passwords.

Third Parties

KeySmith does not share data with any third parties because there is no data to share.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Given that we collect no data, changes are unlikely.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/narissarah/KeySmith.
