KeySmith is a privacy-first password manager that keeps everything on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking — just secure, offline password storage protected by your Keychain.

FEATURES
• Generate strong passwords with customizable length, symbols, and character options
• Passphrase generator with configurable word count and separators
• Strength meter with real-time entropy calculation
• Secure vault with categories and favorites
• PIN + biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID)
• Clipboard auto-clear after 30 seconds
• Custom keyboard extension for quick password access
• Beautiful Liquid Glass design
• Fully offline — zero network connections
• No ads, no telemetry, no data collection

SECURITY
• Passwords stored in iOS Keychain with hardware-level encryption
• PIN protected with PBKDF2 (600,000 iterations)
• Brute-force lockout with persistent attempt tracking
• Complete Data Protection enabled
• AutoFill Credential Provider support

KeySmith is designed for people who believe their passwords should never leave their device. Simple, secure, private.
